H2H Academy · Self-Realization Mentorship

Before you figure out what to do with your life, understand who you are.

Your choices, relationships and direction are influenced by how you see yourself, what you value and what you have accepted about who you are.

SRM is a guided mentorship experience for people seeking greater understanding of themselves, their values, their direction and the way they are choosing to live.

The work

What you will examine.

01

Understand yourself

Explore identity, self-image, talents, skills, upbringing and how you see yourself.

02

Examine what you express

Consider fear, shame, insecurity, confidence, pride, boldness, character and how you communicate your value.

03

See beyond yourself

Explore service, genuine needs and how personal passions can become useful to other people.

04

Understand people & relationships

Examine humanity, identity, relationships, love, accommodation and the way people understand one another.

05

Understand God's love

Explore mercy, forgiveness, mistakes, punishment, help and the role of the Holy Spirit.

The journey

A structured 7-week experience.

Weeks 1–5

Learning

Engage the core ideas and questions that form the foundation of self-realization.

Week 6

Project

Apply the learning through a project that requires engagement with what you have learned.

After project

Examination

Demonstrate your understanding of the journey.

Completion

Closing ceremony

Complete the programme after a deliberate process of examination, learning and application.

Included in SRM

Who it is for

For people who are ready to examine themselves honestly.

SRM requires participation, reflection, honesty and willingness to learn and unlearn. It is not positioned as a service that hands someone a perfect five-year plan.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How long is SRM?

The programme is structured as a 7-week journey with five weeks of learning, one week of project work, followed by examination and a closing ceremony.

Is SRM Christian?

Yes. H2H is a Christian faith-informed enterprise, and SRM engages Christian principles and biblical perspectives as part of its approach.

Do I need to already know my purpose?

No. The programme is designed for individuals seeking greater clarity around identity, values, purpose and life direction.

What is included in the 1-on-1 consultation?

Every enrolled participant receives one free, one-time 1-on-1 consultation with Famous Warrior Chijioke during the final week. The participant decides what to bring to the conversation.

How much is SRM?

The current H2H Enterprise Catalogue lists ₦80,000 for the virtual edition and ₦104,000 for the physical edition.
